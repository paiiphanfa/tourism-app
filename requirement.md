Tourism Application

- This is an app to help tourist who visit Thailand have a guide for their trip. 
- When use open the app, they need to log in and location allowed. 
- What the app can do is that, when they want to travel somewhere in Thailand, they just search up the place or province, the period od the travel, and more if needed and then the app can make a whole plan for them.
    - For example, they search for "Chiang Mai", 3 days stay. the app will plan the 3 days plan for them. 
- Moreover, there is a chat feature that tourist can talk to. Nowsaday, tourists tend to travel alone across the overseas, and sometime they might feel bored, so they can chat with the chatbot, for example, they say "This place is boring" then the bot will answer something and suggest a newplace for them.
